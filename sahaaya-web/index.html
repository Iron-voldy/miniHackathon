Sahaaya helps a non-speaking person (e.g. after a stroke, or living with ALS or cerebral palsy) select a phrase on a board, confirm it, have it spoken aloud in Sinhala, Tamil, or English, and notify a linked caregiver who can acknowledge it. It is not speech therapy, diagnosis, or an emergency service.
